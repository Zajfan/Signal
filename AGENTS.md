# Project workflow

The owner is the sole developer and prefers direct work on `main`. Commit and push completed, verified changes to `main`; do not create feature branches or worktrees unless the owner requests them.

Signal will be hosted on the owner's own machine at `signal.tnhc.dev`. Cloudflare manages the domain; do not infer a preference for Workers/D1 hosting. Keep the development server's identity and simulated payment controls separate from the public runtime.
